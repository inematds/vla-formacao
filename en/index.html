The inflection point

Robotics just had its “ChatGPT moment.”
And almost no one in Brazil has noticed.

In October 2024, Physical Intelligence launched the π0 — the first model trained at internet scale to make a robot fold laundry, assemble boxes, and make coffee without being programmed for each task. In January 2025, a BMW put VLA models into permanent production at a factory. In 2026, Jensen Huang (NVIDIA) stated: the turning point for robotics has already happened — isn’t coming.

The cost of a humanoid fell from US$ 85.000 para US$ 25.000 in two years. A research robotic arm, the SO-101 from HuggingFace, costs ~US$ 100. The best models — OpenVLA, π0, GR00T N1 — are open. For the first time in history, anyone can learn, train, and deploy AI that acts in the physical world.

Global talent race

How countries are training this talent

While governments invest billions in training, Brazil still has no specialized VLA training. This is the space this school fills.

🇨🇳

China — a declared national strategy

  • Robotics and embodied AI listed as a top "new industrial track" in 15th Five-Year Plan (2026–2030).
  • “Robot schools” in Beijing and a data center in Hubei with ~100 humanoids repeating tasks hundreds of times a day to generate training data.
  • Unitree + AgiBot are expected to account for ~80% of the humanoids shipped worldwide in 2026. Unitree G1 costs US$ 16,000.
  • Industrial density: 470 robots / 10,000 workers (3rd in the world) — doubled in 4 years.
🇺🇸

U.S. — academia + capital + defense

  • Stanford, Berkeley, CMU, MIT are the birthplace of the open models (OpenVLA, ALOHA, Octo) used around the world.
  • The NSF funds intelligent robotics (~US$ 70 mi/year); the DoD allocates US$ 10,3 bi autonomy and robotics.
  • Skild AI was founded by CMU professors; Physical Intelligence by former Google DeepMind researchers.
  • Embodied AI salaries reach US$ 335 mil/ano (General Motors, Mountain View).
🇪🇺

Europe / Germany — dual training

  • Horizon Europe: US$ 183.5 million in robotics (2023–2025). High-Tech Strategy 2025 German: US$ 369 mi.
  • Germany is Europe’s largest market (429 robots / 10,000 workers) and is investing in dual vocational training.
  • NEURA Robotics became the most funded European humanoid robotics company: US$ 1,2 bi (Mar 2026).
🇯🇵

Japan & Korea — state policies

  • Japan: "New Robot Strategy" to become the biggest hub for robotics innovation, and the Moonshot R&D (US$ 440 million, by 2050).
  • South Korea is among the 5 countries with the highest industrial robot density in the world (IFR, 2025).
  • Stated focus: manufacturing, healthcare, and an aging population.
